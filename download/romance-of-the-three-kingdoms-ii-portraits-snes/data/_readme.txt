This directory contains four files.

My (James https://kongming.net) aim was to include some of the source information I used to compile the portraits in this archive, given the work of others made this much easier to accomplish, and I wanted to credit their efforts, thank them, and preserve their work.

- - -

1. officer-data.csv
This is the CSV document I tossed together, along with a one-off program I wrote, to extract the portraits from sprites, associate their sequence with Names, Portrait IDs, and in-game Officer IDs, and export named portraits based on in-game data.

I wanted to include it just in case it is helpful to someone else.

- - -

2. sprite-unique.png
3. sprite-generic.png
These are tiled sprites of portraits from Romance of the Three Kingdoms II for SNES that were shared by "Team-Wu" on GameFAQs. One includes all the unique portraits, including daughters and created rulers, and the other all the generic portraits.

Portraits in both of these tiled sprite files appear in the order of in-game Portrait ID, from left to right, top to bottom, and can be looped over by looping the data in officer-data.csv, sorted in ascending order by Portrait ID. XXX values are unique portraits and XXXXX values are generic portraits.

Source: https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/588628-romance-of-the-three-kingdoms-ii/77132502


- - -

2. data-spreadsheet.zip
This is a spreadsheet filled with officer information from the vanilla Romance of the Three Kingdoms II SNES ROM.

I did not author this spreadsheet. It was shared by "Team-Wu" on GameFAQs (see below). Because it is relevant to the portraits in this archive, and on GameFAQs linked via MediaFire, I suspect there is a modest chance that this archive could vanish at some point in the future—at least from this source.

At the time of writing you can find it here:
https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/588628-romance-of-the-three-kingdoms-ii/77132502#message_911168546
