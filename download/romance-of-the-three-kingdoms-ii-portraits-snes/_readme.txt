Romance of the Three Kingdoms II SNES Portraits

Complete collection of portraits from Romance of the Three Kingdoms II (SNES), shared by Kongming’s Archives as a courtesy to fans of the game.

Many thanks to "Team-Wu" and others for sharing information and some extracted data at GameFAQs (see readme in 'data'). Their efforts made it far easier to put this archive of named portraits together than would have otherwise been the case.

If you use these portraits online, please consider linking back to us. Doing so is one small act to help an independent website like ours reach others in an age when the internet is dominated by commercial platforms and LLM-generated content.

https://kongming.net/2/portraits/snes/

It also brings me a spark o' joy in exchange for sharing an archive this with no advertisements and no data collection; just a public button to manifest goodies onto your device.
