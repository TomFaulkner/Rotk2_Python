Country Information

0x0F: Number of rulers

Start address: 0x2AFC, length: 0x29
0x00~0x01: Minus 0x38 is ruler offset
0x02~0x03: Minus 0x38 is province where ruler is located
0x04~0x05: Minus 0x38 is strategist offset
0x06~0x06: Trust level of each country toward me
0x0A~0x0B: 0A bit0 = whether country 0 is allied with me; bit1 = country 1...bit7 = country 7; 0B bit0 = country 8...0B bit7 = country 15.
0x0C~0x0D: magic
calculate:
magic = 0
iterate ruler's every province:
	a=int(troops/100), b=(num of officers)*10
	magic = a+b

0x0E~0x1C: Hostility level of each country toward me
0x21~0x21: marriage
0x22~0x22: FF=normal, 1E=wandering
